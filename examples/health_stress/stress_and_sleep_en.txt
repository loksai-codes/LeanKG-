Stress and sleep (hand-written sample)

Chronic stress increases the risk of insomnia. Work stress is associated with burnout and poor concentration.
Insomnia is often caused by shift work. Depression causes fatigue and loss of appetite.

Regular exercise reduces anxiety in adults. Cognitive behavioral therapy treats insomnia, and mindfulness meditation lowers cortisol levels.
In one small trial, yoga did not reduce blood pressure. Social isolation may increase the risk of depression.

Stress was measured with the Perceived Stress Scale (PSS), and depression with the PHQ-9.
